How we work

From brief to final file

  1. 01

    Brief

    A short call or message: your brand, audience, formats, budget and deadline. We send a written quote and timeline.

  2. 02

    First concept

    We design one clear direction built around a single idea, not ten random options.

  3. 03

    Revisions

    We refine it together with your feedback until it is right, within the rounds in your package.

  4. 04

    Final files

    You get organised, ready-to-use files for social media, web, print or the Play Store.

  5. 05

    Support

    Need a new size, a follow-up post or an app update later? We keep your files and pick up where we left off.

FAQ

Questions

How long does a project take?

A logo or a set of posts usually takes a few days to two weeks; websites and apps take longer. You get a timeline with your quote.

How do I get a quote?

Book a short call below or email us with what you need and when. We reply with a written quote.

Do I own the final files?

Yes. Once the project is paid, the final files are yours to use.

Do you work with clients outside Dhaka?

Yes. Most work happens over calls, email and messages, so we work with clients anywhere.
