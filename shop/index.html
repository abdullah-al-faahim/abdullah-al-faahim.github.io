How ordering works

  1. 01

    Add to cart

    Pick designs, merchandise or ebooks. Choose a size or licence where it applies.

  2. 02

    Send your order

    Share your name, phone and email. No account needed and nothing is charged yet.

  3. 03

    Pay and receive

    We email bKash, Nagad or bank details. Files arrive by email, merchandise is delivered to you.

Digital items are emailed after payment is confirmed, usually within a few hours. Merchandise ships across Bangladesh in 3 to 5 working days.

Questions

Need a custom design, a bulk order or an invoice? Ask the studio or book a call.

Is my card charged when I send the order?

No. Sending an order only tells us what you want. We reply by email with payment details (bKash, Nagad or bank transfer), and you pay after that.

How do I receive digital products?

By email, as a download link, after your payment is confirmed. Ebooks are PDF, design files come as editable Figma or vector files plus PDF previews.

What can I do with a design file?

Personal and commercial use for your own brand or clients is included. You may not resell or redistribute the files themselves. Ask for an extended licence for products you plan to sell.

Can I return merchandise?

Yes, within 7 days if it arrives damaged or the wrong size. Digital files cannot be returned once sent, but write to us if something is wrong and we will fix it.

Do you take custom work?

Yes. Brand identities, social media sets and apps are handled by Utopia SoftTech. Ask for a quote from the Studio page.
